CoD League Rosters

Season 2 rosters, salaries and cap compliance, pulled from Sleeper on Oct 19, 2026, after the Oct 18 free agent auction.

Kept players got the 2nd-season raise: +10%, at least +$1, rounded up. Players won in the auction cost their winning bid. Taxi players get no raise. Cap hit is full salary for active players, half salary (rounded up) for IR and $0 for taxi. The cap is $210, the hard cap is $230, and the buy-in follows the payroll scale in the charter. Injury statuses are as of Oct 19.
