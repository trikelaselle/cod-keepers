How the numbers work. Season 2 salary is last season's salary plus a 10% raise (at least $1), rounded up. Cap hit counts IR players at half their salary (rounded up) and taxi players at $0.

Draft budget is the cap minus the cap hit of everyone you keep. You need $1 for each open roster spot. Spots to fill counts the 15-player active roster and doesn't include IR or taxi.

Anyone you cut goes into the free agent auction, and you can bid on him there. You can change your picks and submit again until the deadline.
