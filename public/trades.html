CoD Trade Machine

Build a trade and check it against both teams' cap numbers. Each team must stay at or under its cap number after the trade. Trading FAAB moves cap room from one team to the other.

How cap numbers work: a team's cap number is the $210 cap, or its payroll at the start of the season if that was higher. During the season, a team's payroll can't go over its cap number. FAAB is a team's room under its cap number. A team that sends $5 of FAAB lowers its own cap number by $5 and raises the other team's by $5. No cap number can go over the $230 hard cap. Cap hit is full salary for active players, half for IR and $0 for taxi; traded players keep their status. This checks the cap only. The commissioner still has to approve and record the trade, including any FAAB.
